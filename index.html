Vesting

See every vest, before it lands.

A private ledger and calendar for RSUs, stock options and ESPP. Gross value and an estimated net after withholding. No account, no server, one-time price.

Features

  • Next vest: date, days left, shares, gross and estimated net.
  • Cliff, monthly, quarterly, yearly and custom schedules. Refreshers stack on top of your first grant.
  • Editable withholding tables for the US, UK, Germany, France, the Netherlands and Türkiye.
  • Scenario slider, PDF and CSV export, vest reminders and a Face ID lock.
  • Side-by-side layout on iPad and the foldable iPhone; tabs on every other iPhone.

Help & FAQ

Does Vesting connect to my broker or employer?

No. You type each grant and the share price yourself. Vesting has no server and never fetches market data.

How is the net value calculated?

Net = gross value − estimated withholding. Withholding uses the rates in Settings → Withholding assumptions, which you can change. The "gap" compares your marginal rate with the income tax withheld. These are estimates, not tax advice.

How are options and ESPP handled?

For options, the value is the spread (price − strike) and the exercise cost is shown separately; ISO exercises are not withheld. For ESPP, the purchase price applies the discount, with an optional look-back to the offering-start price, and withholding applies to the discount.

What does Vesting Pro include?

Unlimited grants and employers, the scenario slider, PDF/CSV export, and country withholding tables. Choose a one-time Lifetime purchase or a yearly subscription. The free version tracks one grant.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Vesting.
