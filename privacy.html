Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Vesting: RSU & Equity Tracker is a personal ledger and calculator for employee equity grants. It is designed so that your grants, share prices and tax assumptions never leave your device.

Your grants and figures

Everything you enter — employers, grants, share prices, schedules, withholding rates and notes — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Vesting has no account system and no server. It does not connect to any bank, broker, employer or market-data service, and we do not upload, read, analyse or keep copies of your data.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup can include Vesting data. That backup is stored under your own account and Apple's terms. If you delete a grant, it is deleted from the app. If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it, except for PDF or CSV files you exported yourself.

Face ID, reminders and widget

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS; Vesting never receives biometric data. Vest reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The Home Screen widget reads a short summary of your next vest from storage shared only between the app and its widget on your device.

Purchases

Vesting Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app, your purchase receipt from Apple, the app version, device model and country or region. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase and is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No grant, salary, share-price or tax data
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts, location or photos
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Not advice

Vesting records what you enter and does arithmetic. Its figures are estimates and are not investment, tax or legal advice.

Children

Vesting does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
